## Found while moving splits to integer money (2026-11-09)

- In-app notification records are not stored: `NotificationService.storeNotificationInFirestore` writes `users/{email_at_domain}/notifications/...` from the client, and the deployed stage 1 rules allow a user's subcollections only to the owner by uid (the email-keyed path never matches), so every write is denied ("Failed to store notification ... permission-denied", silent in release builds). No screen reads those records and nothing sends push from the client, so the practical impact today is nil, but the notification feature needs the Cloud Functions work (Phase 2c) and the stage 1 rules comment ("no client access, the server uses the Admin SDK") is wrong about who writes them.
- Group and friend balances can differ from before by a few paise: where a total cannot be divided evenly (for example 100.01 between two people), the old screens showed each person half a paisa too much and the shares did not add up; the shares now add up exactly and the extra paisa goes to the first participant. On a comparison over all live groups (11 groups, 737 usable splits) no split's shares failed to sum to its total, 4 of 20 pair balances differed from the old float result by more than half a paisa, and the largest difference was 3.67 paise.
- Still open on the delete path: deleting a settlement entry restores only the deleting user's own balance; the other member's balance (changed by the cross-user increment when the settlement was recorded) is not reversed, and the separate `groups/*/settlements` record is not removed. Deleting a regular split likewise does not touch other members' balances (they have no transaction for it). The balance reconciliation report needs to account for this, and the Cloud Functions work (Phase 2c) should own balance changes that cross users.
- Settlement delete is now handled by the `deleteSettlement` callable (Phase 2c): it reverses both balances and removes the linked settlement record and both people's transactions. Not covered: settlements whose split entry an old app already deleted (the settlement record and the other person's balance change stay as they are), and balances already off from earlier deletes. Needs the functions deployed before the new app ships. Deleting a regular split still changes only the deleting user's own balance, which is correct because other members have no transaction for it.
- Group edit bug, confirmed on 2026-11-09 and fixed (`src/utils/groupEdit.ts`): saving an edit of a group in `SplitScreen.tsx` wrote `members: [editor]`, `pendingInvites: everyone ticked` and `createdBy: editor`, with no invitations. Saving without any change removed every other member from `members` (they became pending with nothing to accept), and a non-creator editing took over `createdBy`. The app now keeps current members who stay ticked, keeps pending invitations, invites only genuinely new people with real invitations, and never rewrites `createdBy`. Old installed apps still have the bug until the store release. Damage assessment on the 2026-11-09 08:49 export: of 22 groups, exactly one showed the signature (only the creator in `members`, four people pending, no invitation documents for them): the group `h4g4mjGBjnkkqkdgIy4r` ("Chennai"), last edited 2025-11-16. With the owner's approval it was repaired on 2026-11-09: three people with accepted invitations or many splits in the group were put back into `members`, and the fourth, who was never invited, got a real pending invitation. Copy of the group document before the repair: the audit folder, `repair-backups/group-h4g4mjGBjnkkqkdgIy4r-before.json` (to undo: set `members` and `pendingInvites` back to that copy and delete the invitation created that day for that group). Not detectable from the data: a `createdBy` overwritten by a non-creator who edited, and groups where everyone has since re-accepted. Check again against a newer export before the backfill.
- Fixed in the same change: deleting a split removed the payer's transaction without restoring their balance, so every deleted split left that balance too low by the split amount. Existing balances of users who already deleted splits are not repaired; the balance reconciliation report (Phase 2a) will show how many.

- The scan limits are 20 a person a day and 900 a month for the whole app (`scanLimits/*`, under Vision's 1,000 a month free tier); check the Cloud Vision usage in the console after the first weeks.
